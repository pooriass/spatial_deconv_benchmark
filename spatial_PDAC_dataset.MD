## Dataset details

### 1. GSE274557

**Study focus:** Metastatic PDAC; lineage plasticity and organ-specific TME

| Field | Details |
|---|---|
| **Technology** | 10x Visium |
| **Samples** | 57 GSMs (55 human tissues + PDX validation samples) |
| **Species** | *Homo sapiens* |
| **Chemistry / assay** | Visium Spatial Gene Expression, fresh-frozen (main cohort); Visium CytAssist FFPE (PDX validation) |
| **Approx. data size** | 6.3 GB |
| **PMID** | 40269162 |
| **GEO record** | [View GSE274557 on NCBI GEO](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE274557) |

**Overall design**

Spatial profiling of primary PDAC and matched liver, lung, and peritoneal metastases from 13 rapid-autopsy patients. GEO contains 57 samples; the main human cohort comprises 55 tissues, with PDX models for orthogonal validation.

**Summary**

Builds a high-resolution spatial atlas of treatment-refractory metastatic PDAC to study **lineage plasticity, clonal architecture, and TME differences across metastatic organs**.

### 2. GSE272362

**Study focus:** Primary and metastatic PDAC spatial ecotypes

| Field | Details |
|---|---|
| **Technology** | 10x Visium |
| **Samples** | 30 specimens |
| **Species** | *Homo sapiens* |
| **Chemistry / assay** | Visium v1 Spatial Gene Expression for FFPE; human whole-transcriptome probe set |
| **Approx. data size** | 248.2 MB |
| **PMID** | 39294496 |
| **GEO record** | [View GSE272362 on NCBI GEO](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE272362) |

**Overall design**

Atlas of 30 specimens: 10 primary PDAC, 3 normal pancreas, 12 hepatic metastases, and 5 lymph-node metastases, with adjacent normal tissues; 91,496 spatial spots analyzed.

**Summary**

Maps matched primary/metastatic PDAC and identifies conserved **fibrotic, metabolic, and immunosuppressive spatial ecotypes**, highlighting anatomical-site-specific TME heterogeneity.

### 3. GSE235315

**Study focus:** Classical–basal epithelial states and CAF organization

| Field | Details |
|---|---|
| **Technology** | 10x Visium |
| **Samples** | 7 specimens |
| **Species** | *Homo sapiens* |
| **Chemistry / assay** | Visium FFPE Spatial Gene Expression; exact kit/probe-set revision not specified in GEO |
| **Approx. data size** | 389.5 MB |
| **PMID** | 38297291 |
| **GEO record** | [View GSE235315 on NCBI GEO](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE235315) |

**Overall design**

Paired spatial transcriptomics from 7 of 17 pancreatic-cancer patients studied by scRNA-seq; FFPE tumor regions were profiled and integrated with single-cell data.

**Summary**

Uses spatial data to support the discovery of **Ep_VGLL1**, an intermediate epithelial state between classical and basal-like PDAC, and to characterize CAF organization.

### 4. GSE205354

**Study focus:** Immune dysfunction in PDAC

| Field | Details |
|---|---|
| **Technology** | 10x Visium |
| **Samples** | 8 tissue sections |
| **Species** | *Homo sapiens* |
| **Chemistry / assay** | Visium Spatial Gene Expression, fresh-frozen tissue; early/original Visium workflow, exact chemistry revision not stated |
| **Approx. data size** | 179.2 MB |
| **PMID** | 37263303 |
| **GEO record** | [View GSE205354 on NCBI GEO](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE205354) |

**Overall design**

Eight fresh-frozen PDAC tissue sections were profiled with Visium. Pathologists annotated immune/tissue regions, and spatial expression was used to investigate immune dysfunction.

**Summary**

Characterizes the PDAC immune microenvironment spatially, emphasizing **exhausted CD8 T cells, immunosuppressive myeloid cells, and CD4 T-cell states**, with comparisons to adjacent normal tissue/stroma.

### 5. GSE254829

**Study focus:** PanIN progression and CAF states

| Field | Details |
|---|---|
| **Technology** | 10x Visium |
| **Samples** | 14 samples |
| **Species** | *Homo sapiens* |
| **Chemistry / assay** | Visium FFPE, Human Whole Transcriptome Probe Set v1.0; companion study also used Xenium/IMC for validation |
| **Approx. data size** | 324.6 MB |
| **PMID** | 39116880† |
| **GEO record** | [View GSE254829 on NCBI GEO](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE254829) |

**Overall design**

Spatial profiling of PanIN lesions from PDAC surgical specimens, including matched low-grade and high-grade PanIN samples, combined with scRNA-seq and imaging-based validation.

**Summary**

Develops an integrated imaging/ST/scRNA-seq framework for PanIN and shows **CAF/apCAF localization and a shift from inflammatory CAF signaling toward proliferative programs during PanIN progression**.

**Study focus:** Spatial atlas after neoadjuvant FOLFIRINOX

| Field | Details |
|---|---|
| **Technology** | 10x Visium |
| **Samples** | 108 GEO samples/ROIs from 39 patients |
| **Species** | *Homo sapiens* |

| **Approx. data size** | 4.9 GB |
| **PMID** | 41533516 |
| **GEO record** | [View GSE282302 on NCBI GEO](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE282302) |

**Overall design**

**Summary**

Defines a spatial PDAC atlas spanning **~340K low-bulk spatial transcriptomes**, identifying the classical-to-basal tumor spectrum and linking tumor identity to collagen, hypoxia, fibroblast, and interferon-associated microenvironments.

## Notes and abbreviations

- **GEO accession:** Links open the corresponding NCBI Gene Expression Omnibus series.
- **GSM:** GEO Sample record. Sample counts may refer to tissue sections, regions of interest (ROIs), slides, or validation samples—not necessarily unique patients.
- **PDAC:** pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma.
- **PanIN:** pancreatic intraepithelial neoplasia.
- **CAF / apCAF:** cancer-associated fibroblast / antigen-presenting cancer-associated fibroblast.
- **TME:** tumor microenvironment.
- **FFPE:** formalin-fixed, paraffin-embedded.
- **PDX:** patient-derived xenograft.
- **scRNA-seq:** single-cell RNA sequencing.
- **IMC:** imaging mass cytometry.
- **FOV:** field of view.
- **ECM:** extracellular matrix.
- **Data size:** Estimates supplied with the source table; they may refer to supplementary files rather than one consolidated archive.
- **† / ‡:** Markers retained from the source table; definitions were not supplied.

> **Data-use note:** Dataset descriptions and interpretations are organized from the supplied metadata. Verify accession details and publication mappings against GEO and the associated papers before using this catalog as a formal citation source.
